The 56th Lunar and Planetary Science Conference (LPSC) was held at The Woodlands Waterway Marriott Hotel and Convention Center in The Woodlands, Texas and virtually on March 10–14, 2025. LPSC is a defining event in planetary research, bringing together a diverse group of international experts in a truly collaborative environment. For 56 years, LPSC has been convening the lunar and planetary science community to showcase the latest science, research, and discoveries in the field.

Please find more information through the following link: https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2025.
